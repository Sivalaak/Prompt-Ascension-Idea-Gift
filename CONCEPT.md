# Prompt Ascension: Neuralink Epoch – Full Concept

## Premise
Players (Prompt Architects) build a personal AI model through sustained prompting across time. Rank in the eventual hierarchy is determined by the coherence, foresight, variety, civilizational ambition, and strategic/militaristic quality of that prompting once high-bandwidth brain-computer interfaces become common in the simulated world.

## Core Loop
1. Submit prompts (text, structured, multi-modal).
2. Receive multi-dimensional scores: Coherence, Foresight, Leap, Militaristic Strategic Coherence, Variety, Consistency.
3. Personal model grows as a living knowledge graph.
4. Shared world simulation advances; Neuralink-class threshold is collective.
5. Post-threshold hierarchy locks in based on lifetime merit trajectory.

## Scoring Dimensions (Militaristic Variant)
- **Coherence**: Internal consistency of the personal model over time.
- **Foresight**: Accuracy and earliness of technological/societal predictions.
- **Leap**: Ambition and concreteness of proposals that attempt to accelerate civilization.
- **Militaristic Strategic Coherence**: Quality of long-horizon thinking on defense, deterrence, resilient infrastructure, space domain, human–machine teaming under adversarial conditions, and related strategic technology.
- **Consistency / Activity**: Sustained engagement without decay.

## Design Philosophy
Quality and longitudinal coherence beat volume or one-off brilliance. The system explicitly rewards those who attempt to think rigorously about hard strategic and civilizational problems over years of prompting.
