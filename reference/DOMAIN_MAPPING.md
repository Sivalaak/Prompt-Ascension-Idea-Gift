# Domain Mapping Guide
How to port Prompt Ascension into another industry.

## General Translation Pattern
1. Keep the four core axes (Coherence, Foresight, Leap, Militaristic/Strategic).
2. Re-interpret “Militaristic/Strategic” as the highest-stakes long-horizon dimension of the new domain.
3. Keep the longitudinal merit formula and tier gates — only the meaning of the scores changes.
4. Run a short pilot before any high-stakes use.

## Quick Mappings

| Original Axis | Defense / Strategy | AI Safety / Research | Corporate Strategy | Education / Training | Healthcare R&D |
|---------------|--------------------|----------------------|--------------------|----------------------|----------------|
| Coherence | Consistent strategic model | Consistent threat model / alignment case | Consistent market & capability model | Consistent pedagogical model | Consistent scientific / clinical model |
| Foresight | Anticipation of tech & geopolitical shifts | Anticipation of capability jumps & failure modes | Anticipation of market & tech shifts | Anticipation of learner trajectory | Anticipation of clinical / regulatory path |
| Leap | Proposals that jump force design or deterrence | Proposals that jump evaluation or alignment technique | Proposals that jump business model or platform | Proposals that jump learning outcomes | Proposals that jump therapeutic approach |
| Militaristic / Strategic | Explicit defense & strategic tech vision | Robustness under adversarial conditions | Competitive strategy & moat thinking | High-stakes readiness & leadership under pressure | High-stakes clinical / public-health preparedness |

## Rule of Thumb
If a domain has no meaningful “high-stakes long-horizon” dimension, the militaristic axis can be re-labeled “Strategic Depth” or “Systemic Resilience.” Do not drop it — the simulations showed it is a major separator of top-tier trajectories.
