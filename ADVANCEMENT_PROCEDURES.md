# Advancement Procedures

Formal rank tiers are gated by multi-factor criteria after the Neuralink threshold is crossed in the simulation.

## Tiers (highest to lowest)

| Tier | Requirements (approximate) |
|------|----------------------------|
| **Epoch Strategos** | Merit ≥ 0.82 + high final militaristic score (≥ 0.75) + very high consistency (≥ 0.85) |
| **High Command Architect** | Merit ≥ 0.72 + solid militaristic score (≥ 0.65) |
| **Strategic Catalyst** | Merit ≥ 0.60 |
| **Tactical Visionary** | Merit ≥ 0.48 |
| **Field Promoter** | Merit ≥ 0.35 |
| **Initiate** | Below threshold |

## Merit Formula (Militaristic Variant)
Weighted combination of:
- Longitudinal trajectory (coherence + foresight + leap + militaristic)
- Final coherence
- Early foresight (first ~40 months weighted higher)
- Early and final militaristic strategic scores
- Activity / consistency ratio

These procedures are designed to make rank reflect sustained, high-quality strategic prompting rather than short-term performance spikes.
